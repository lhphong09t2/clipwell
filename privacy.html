Privacy Policy — Clipwell Chrome Extension

Last Updated: November 21, 2026 · Publisher: FlowBench Lab

1. Zero Page Telemetry or Remote Scraping

Clipwell processes webpage DOM content, HTML/ARIA tables, and AI chat threads 100% locally inside your browser. Using only Chrome's activeTab and scripting permissions, Clipwell inspects the currently active tab exclusively when you click the Clipwell extension icon and never transmits clipped text, URLs, or tables to any developer server or third-party analytics endpoint.

2. Local Browser Storage (chrome.storage.local)

  • Research Clip Queue (clipwell_bundle_queue_v1): Pages you explicitly choose to add while building a Pro Markdown bundle. You can clear this queue at any time inside the Batch & Pro tab.
  • Pro License State (clipwell_pro_license_v1): Optional Polar.sh license key, activation ID, and validation timestamp stored on your device to enable 12-hour revalidation and a 7-day offline grace window.

3. Optional License Validation (https://api.polar.sh)

If you enter a Clipwell Pro license key ($29 one-time purchase), Clipwell sends only the license key, Clipwell's Polar organization identifier, and extension activation label directly over HTTPS to Polar.sh (https://api.polar.sh/v1/customer-portal/license-keys) to activate or validate your key. Free users who do not enter a key trigger zero external network requests.

4. Data Sale & Tracking Certification

  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We do not use user data for purposes unrelated to Clipwell's single clipping purpose or for creditworthiness/lending.
  • We do not embed remote executable code, tracking pixels, cookies, or ad SDKs.
